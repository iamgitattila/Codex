## RISKS AND MITIGATION

### Risk 1: Legal/Regulatory
**Risk:** Some content (biometric defeat, black markets) could face legal challenges or platform bans.

**Mitigation:**
- Include thorough legal disclaimers
- Frame content as educational/informational
- Diversify sales platforms (own site + third-party)
- Consult legal counsel on high-risk titles
- Use geo-restriction if necessary

### Risk 2: Market Saturation
**Risk:** 200 books might overwhelm buyers, leading to decision paralysis.

**Mitigation:**
- Strong curation through bundles
- Archetype-based recommendation quizzes
- Clear starter paths for different customer types
- Free samples/chapters to prove value
- Comprehensive guides to the catalog

### Risk 3: Production Bandwidth
**Risk:** 40 new books is substantial content development.

**Mitigation:**
- Phase releases (5-10 books per quarter)
- Use outlined structure for faster drafting
- Consider co-authors for specialized topics
- Maintain quality standards (don't rush)
- Bundle existing content during new book production

### Risk 4: Platform Dependence
**Risk:** Email platform or payment processor could ban controversial content.

**Mitigation:**
- Build email list across multiple platforms
- Own customer data (download regularly)
- Multiple payment processors (Stripe, PayPal, crypto)
- Self-hosted sales pages as backup
- Physical product fallbacks
